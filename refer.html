Why refer to Arcline

  • Your client is looked after from design to handover.
  • 10-year warranty on interiors.
  • Referral terms are clear and in writing.
  • You get updates on your referral's progress.
  1. A referral is valid only if the client's details are submitted before they contact Arcline themselves, and they have not been in discussion with us earlier.
  2. The bonus applies only when the referred client signs an execution agreement with Arcline within 3 months of the referral. Design-only projects do not qualify.
  3. The bonus is a percentage of the execution value received by Arcline, excluding taxes. The rate is confirmed in writing to the referrer on WhatsApp after the referral is accepted and before the client signs.

  6. One referrer is credited per client. If two people refer the same client, the first logged submission counts.
  7. Referred clients get a free consultation and site visit. The free design offer does not apply.
  8. You cannot claim the bonus on your own project.
  9. Taxes on the bonus are the referrer's responsibility.
  10. Arcline may change or end the program at any time, but will not change terms for referrals already accepted.
  11. Arcline's decision on bonus eligibility is final.
